### Overview [0:12]
**Transcript:** Ostensibly, the Content Library looks like bridge, but it has a major distinction.  The library is interactive with Dash's tools and includes many handy ways of speeding up  your workflow inside of Unreal Engine.  Start by opening Dash.  With Dash open, the BIcon will allow you to open the Content Library.  Any Megascans assets that you've downloaded will be available here to browse, filter,  or drop into your environment.

**Frame:** tutorials\frames\beginner-content-library-tutorial-for-ue5\frame_001.jpg

### Open Content Library [0:35]
**Transcript:** In time, the Content Library will also feature assets from other Content Libraries or assets  that you've personally created.  You can search inside of the Content Library by typing in the text field at the top, making  it easier to find specifically what you're looking to work with.

**Frame:** tutorials\frames\beginner-content-library-tutorial-for-ue5\frame_002.jpg

### Scale Assets [0:51]
**Transcript:** Dragging the mouse while left-clicking will move the selected asset across the surface  of the mesh below it.  There's other options as well, which you can see demonstrated here in real time.  You can scale the asset by holding Control and left dragging.  Shift dragging will rotate the mesh.  Control Shift and left mouse will scale along the normal direction of the asset.  Control and right mouse button will scale the width and height of the object while shift  in right mouse button will rotate the mesh outside of its normal direction.  The other options are for decals and don't apply to the meshes that we're using here  in this scene.

**Frame:** tutorials\frames\beginner-content-library-tutorial-for-ue5\frame_003.jpg

### Surface Scatter [1:23]
**Transcript:** You can also scatter objects by holding Control and dragging them into the environment,  which brings up the Surface Scatter menu.  Now, we're going to cover Surface Scatter in more depth in a later video, but for now,  here's a preview of what you can do with it.

**Frame:** tutorials\frames\beginner-content-library-tutorial-for-ue5\frame_004.jpg

### Physics [1:54]
**Transcript:** You can also place objects via physics using Dash by holding Control while you're dragging  an object into the environment.  We're going to cover this in depth later on in another video, too.  We can move and place decals with the same controls as well, not to mention dragging  and dropping surfaces onto objects, too.  This intuitive asset placement in Dash makes working with Unreal Engine much easier and  significantly more user friendly.  You'll spend less time fiddling with asset placement and more time being in the zone.  For now, I'm Jonathan, Community Director, and I'll see you next time with a new video.  Thanks for watching, guys.
